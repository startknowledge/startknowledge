What is German Patriotism?

German patriotism is a complex and multifaceted concept that encompasses a range of emotions, attitudes, and behaviors. However, it can also involve a sense of duty and responsibility to contribute to the well-being and prosperity of the nation. In recent years, there has been a growing movement to redefine German patriotism and make it more inclusive and diverse, recognizing the contributions and experiences of people from all backgrounds (source: Bertelsmann Stiftung, 2019).
