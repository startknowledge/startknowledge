Introduction to the MCA Knowledge Centre

The MCA knowledge centre is designed to serve as a one-stop platform for businesses, providing them with access to a wide range of resources, including data, research reports, and expert advice. The centre will focus on areas such as corporate governance, compliance, and regulatory affairs, with the objective of empowering businesses to navigate the complex regulatory landscape and make informed decisions. As noted by the MCA (2020), the knowledge centre will also provide training and capacity-building programs for corporate professionals, enabling them to stay updated on the latest developments and best practices in the field.
