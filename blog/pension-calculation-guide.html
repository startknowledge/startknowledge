Indian Army Pension Calculation – Complete Guide

Pension calculation is one of the most searched topics among Army personnel. Understanding the calculation method helps in retirement planning and financial security.

What is Army Pension?

Army pension is a monthly payment given to retired defence personnel after completing qualifying service.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Minimum 20 years of qualifying service
  • Medical retirement cases may have special rules

How is Pension Calculated?

Pension is generally calculated as 50% of last drawn basic pay.

Formula:

Pension = (Last Basic Pay × 50%)

Example

If last basic pay is ₹40,000:

Pension = 40,000 × 50% = ₹20,000 per month

Other Benefits

  • Dearness Relief (DR)
  • Gratuity
  • Leave encashment

Important Tips

  • Check service records carefully
  • Verify rank pay and MSP inclusion
