Introduction to the MCA Knowledge Centre

The MCA knowledge centre is designed to serve as a **one-stop-shop** for entrepreneurs, offering a wide range of resources, including **market research reports**, **industry trends**, and **best practices**. The centre will provide access to **experts and mentors**, who will guide entrepreneurs through the various stages of setting up and running a business. With the **rising number of startups in India**, which has grown by **10% in the last year** (Source: Startup India, 2022), the MCA knowledge centre is poised to play a vital role in supporting the growth of the entrepreneurial ecosystem.
