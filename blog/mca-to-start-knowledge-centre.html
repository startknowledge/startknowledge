Introduction to the MCA Knowledge Centre

The MCA knowledge centre is expected to serve as a hub for businesses, providing them with access to a wide range of resources, including data, research reports, and expert advice. The centre will focus on areas such as corporate governance, compliance, and regulatory affairs, with the goal of empowering businesses to make informed decisions and drive growth. As noted by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (2020), the knowledge centre will play a critical role in promoting transparency and accountability in the corporate sector.

There are several examples of successful knowledge centres around the world, which can serve as a model for the MCA knowledge centre. Similarly, the Commonwealth Business Council has launched a knowledge centre in London, which offers resources and support to businesses operating in Commonwealth countries.

In India, the National Stock Exchange (NSE) has established a knowledge centre, which provides training and capacity-building programs for corporate professionals.
