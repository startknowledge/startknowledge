Privacy Policy

This page explains the general privacy approach used by StartKnowledge across its websites, tools and digital projects.

1. Introduction

StartKnowledge respects the privacy of visitors and aims to handle website information responsibly. This Privacy Policy describes the general types of information that may be processed when visitors access StartKnowledge websites, use online tools, communicate with the team or interact with third-party services integrated into a project.

Different StartKnowledge projects may have different technical architectures. A static informational page may process considerably less information than an application that uses an API or external service. Therefore, visitors should also review the privacy documentation of a specific third-party service where applicable.

2. Information You Provide

Visitors may voluntarily provide information when they contact StartKnowledge by email, submit feedback or otherwise communicate with the team. Depending on the purpose of communication, this may include a name, email address, message contents or technical information voluntarily included by the visitor.

Visitors should not send passwords, payment-card details, private API keys, authentication tokens or unnecessary confidential information through ordinary email.

3. Automatically Processed Information

Web servers, hosting providers, analytics platforms and security systems may automatically process technical information such as IP addresses, browser characteristics, device information, referring pages, timestamps and requested resources. Such information can be used for security, performance monitoring, diagnostics and aggregated website analytics.

4. Analytics

StartKnowledge may use analytics technologies such as Google Analytics to understand general website usage, traffic patterns, page performance and technical behaviour. Analytics services may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies.

5. Advertising

Some StartKnowledge websites may display advertisements through third-party advertising networks, including Google AdSense. Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure or personalize advertising according to their own policies and applicable settings.

6. Cookies

Cookies and similar technologies may be used for website functionality, preferences, analytics, security or advertising. The exact cookies used can depend on the services enabled on a particular page.

More information is available on the Cookies Policy.

7. Third-Party Services

StartKnowledge projects may link to or integrate with third-party services, including hosting platforms, analytics services, advertising providers, AI services, APIs and external websites. These providers operate under their own policies and terms.

StartKnowledge cannot control every aspect of data processing performed by an external provider. Users should review the relevant provider's privacy documentation before using an external service.

8. AI Services and APIs

Selected applications may use artificial intelligence or external APIs. Information sent to such services depends on the implementation of the specific application. Users should avoid submitting confidential or sensitive information unless the application explicitly provides an appropriate secure mechanism.

9. External Project Websites

The StartKnowledge ecosystem includes independent project websites such as the Ultra Static SEO Engine, YouTube Video Intelligence Analyzer, Calculator Library Portal, BN Ration Scale and Pension Calculator.

Other projects may include AI Mosaic Studio, Design Painting, Motionix and Automation Workspace.

10. Email Communication

Visitors can contact the team through:

  • contact@startknowledge.in
  • support@startknowledge.in
  • hello@startknowledge.in

11. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures may be used to protect information under our control. However, no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Children's Privacy

The websites are not designed to intentionally collect unnecessary personal information from children. Parents or guardians should supervise children's use of online services where appropriate.

13. External Links

StartKnowledge contains links to external websites. Visiting an external website takes the visitor outside StartKnowledge's direct control. External websites may have different privacy practices.

14. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated when website functionality, third-party services, legal requirements or technical practices change. The latest version published on this page should be treated as the current version.

15. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to contact@startknowledge.in .

Important: This Privacy Policy provides general website information and should not be interpreted as legal advice. Specific projects may have additional privacy requirements or notices.
