Retirement Planning in India – Step by Step Guide

Retirement planning helps you live a stress-free life after leaving your job. Starting early makes a huge difference.

Why Retirement Planning is Important?

  • Financial independence
  • Medical security
  • Peaceful lifestyle

How Much Should You Save?

A general rule is to save at least 25–30 times your annual expenses.

Best Investment Options

  • PF & PPF
  • NPS
  • Fixed Deposits
  • Mutual Funds

Simple Example

If your monthly expense is ₹25,000, annual = ₹3,00,000

Target retirement fund = ₹75–90 lakh
