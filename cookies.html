Cookies Policy

Information about cookies and similar technologies that may be used across StartKnowledge websites and applications.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of information stored by a website or service on a user's device. They can help websites remember preferences, maintain functionality, understand usage patterns or support advertising.

2. Why Cookies May Be Used

StartKnowledge may use cookies or similar technologies for several purposes, including website functionality, security, analytics, performance measurement, preferences and advertising.

3. Essential Technologies

Some technologies may be necessary for a website or application to operate correctly. Disabling certain functionality can affect the user experience.

4. Analytics Cookies

StartKnowledge may use analytics services such as Google Analytics. Analytics technologies can help understand aggregate traffic, popular pages, technical performance and general visitor behaviour.

5. Advertising Cookies

Some pages may display advertisements through services such as Google AdSense. Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies and user settings.

6. Third-Party Cookies

External services embedded into or linked from StartKnowledge may place their own cookies. StartKnowledge does not control every cookie created by an independent third-party service.

7. Managing Cookies

Most modern browsers provide controls for viewing, blocking or deleting cookies. Browser settings can affect website functionality when cookies are disabled.

8. Privacy

Cookies may be associated with technical or usage information. For broader information about privacy and data handling, see the Privacy Policy.
