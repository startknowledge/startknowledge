Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about StartKnowledge, its calculators, technical projects, AI features, SEO systems, advertising and support.

1. What is StartKnowledge?

StartKnowledge is a digital knowledge and tools platform containing calculators, informational resources, technical projects, AI-assisted applications and other practical online utilities.

2. Is StartKnowledge the same as every project listed on the website?

The projects belong to the broader StartKnowledge ecosystem, but individual applications can have separate domains, technical architectures and functions.

3. Are the calculators official government calculators?

Not automatically. Unless a particular page explicitly identifies itself as an official government service, calculator results should be treated as informational estimates or calculations.

4. Can I use the Pension Calculator for official pension decisions?

The pension calculator is intended as a planning and estimation tool. Official pension benefits depend on applicable rules, records and individual circumstances. Users should verify important results with the relevant department or authority.

5. What is the BN Ration Scale project?

BN Ration Scale is a digital tool focused on ration-scale calculations and related informational resources.

6. What is the Calculator Library Portal?

The Calculator Library Portal brings different categories of online calculators and utility tools into one accessible platform.

7. Does StartKnowledge use artificial intelligence?

Selected projects may use AI for text generation, analysis, classification, creative tasks or other supported workflows. AI output can contain errors and should be independently verified where accuracy matters.

8. Can AI-generated information be considered professional advice?

No. Unless specifically stated otherwise, AI-assisted output is informational and should not replace professional legal, financial, medical, investment or governmental advice.

9. Does StartKnowledge guarantee Google rankings?

No. SEO tools and workflows cannot guarantee search rankings, indexing, traffic, backlinks or revenue. Search-engine algorithms and policies are controlled by search engines.

10. Does the website use Google Analytics?

The main website may use Google Analytics to understand website usage, traffic and performance. See the Privacy Policy and Cookies Policy for additional information.

11. Does StartKnowledge display advertisements?

Some pages may display advertising through third-party advertising providers such as Google AdSense.

12. Are affiliate links used?

Some pages may contain affiliate links. If an eligible visitor action results in compensation, StartKnowledge may receive an affiliate commission.

13. Does clicking an affiliate link cost extra?

Normally, an affiliate commission does not require an additional charge from the visitor, but the terms and pricing of the third-party merchant always apply.

14. Why are external websites linked from StartKnowledge?

External links can provide access to independent StartKnowledge projects, third-party documentation, services, tools or additional information.

15. Does StartKnowledge control third-party websites?

No. External websites and APIs can change independently, and visitors should review their own terms and privacy policies.

16. What should I do if a calculator gives an unexpected result?

Check the values entered, units, assumptions and applicable rules. If the problem appears to be a software issue, contact support with the tool name and a description of the problem.

17. How can I contact StartKnowledge?

General contact: contact@startknowledge.in. Technical support: support@startknowledge.in. General communication: hello@startknowledge.in.

18. Does StartKnowledge store everything users enter into calculators?

Not necessarily. Data handling depends on the implementation of the specific tool. Visitors should review the applicable privacy information and avoid entering unnecessary confidential information.

19. Can I report a broken link?

Yes. Broken links and technical problems can be reported to support@startknowledge.in.

20. Where can I read more?

Read the About, Privacy Policy, Terms, Disclaimer, Cookies Policy, Documentation and Support pages.
