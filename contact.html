What to Include in a Support Request

When reporting a technical problem, providing useful information can make troubleshooting faster. A support message can include the name of the project, the page or tool involved, a short description of the problem, the approximate time when the problem occurred, the browser or device being used and, where appropriate, a non-sensitive screenshot.

Do not send passwords, payment-card information, authentication tokens, private keys or other confidential credentials by email. If a technical problem can be reproduced without exposing sensitive information, explain the steps that produced the problem instead.

Privacy reminder: Please do not include unnecessary sensitive or confidential information in support requests. Only provide information needed to understand the issue.

External Services

Some StartKnowledge projects may use external APIs, hosting platforms, analytics services, advertising networks, AI providers or other third-party infrastructure. Problems caused by an external service may not be directly controlled by StartKnowledge.

If your issue involves an external service, include the relevant public error message or non-sensitive description. Do not provide API keys, passwords or private authentication information.
