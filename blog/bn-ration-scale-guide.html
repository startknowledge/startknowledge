BN Ration Scale – Complete Guide (Eligibility, Calculation & Benefits)

BN Ration Scale is an important entitlement system for Indian Army personnel. Many soldiers and families often get confused about how ration scale is calculated, who is eligible, and how much quantity is allowed. In this guide, we will explain everything in simple and practical language. Read more

What is BN Ration Scale?

BN Ration Scale refers to the fixed quantity of ration items that are issued to Army personnel on a daily or monthly basis. These rations are provided to ensure proper nutrition and health of serving soldiers and their dependents. Read more

Who is Eligible for BN Ration?

  • Serving Army personnel
  • JCOs and ORs
  • Personnel living in field or peace stations
  • In some cases, dependents as per rules

BN Ration Scale Items

Common items included in BN ration scale: Read more

  • Rice / Wheat / Atta
  • Dal
  • Cooking Oil
  • Sugar
  • Tea / Coffee
  • Salt and spices

How is BN Ration Scale Calculated?

Ration is calculated based on daily entitlement per person. For example, if one soldier is entitled to 600g of rice per day, then monthly requirement will be: Read more

600g × 30 days = 18 kg per month Read more

Example Calculation

If a unit has 100 soldiers and each is entitled to 18 kg rice monthly: Read more

100 × 18 = 1800 kg rice per month Read more

Important Points

  • Ration scale differs for field and peace areas.
  • Quantities may change as per government orders.
  • Always verify from unit supply office.

FAQs

Is BN ration available to family members?

Only in selected cases, depending on posting and entitlement category. Read more

Does ration scale change yearly?

Changes happen based on government policies, not yearly. Read more
