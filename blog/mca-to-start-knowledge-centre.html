MCA to Start Knowledge Centre: Empowering Entrepreneurs with Actionable Strategies

The Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) has taken a significant step towards promoting entrepreneurship and corporate governance in the country by announcing the launch of a knowledge centre. This initiative is expected to provide a platform for entrepreneurs, corporates, and stakeholders to access relevant information, resources, and expertise. Introduction to the Knowledge Centre

The MCA's knowledge centre aims to bridge this gap by providing a comprehensive platform for entrepreneurs to access information, resources, and expertise.
