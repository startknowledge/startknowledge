Introduction to the Knowledge Centre

The MCA knowledge centre is envisioned as a one-stop destination for entrepreneurs and businesses to access information, resources, and expertise related to company law, corporate governance, and other relevant areas. The centre will provide a range of services, including training and capacity-building programs, research and analysis, and advisory services. The knowledge centre will also serve as a platform for networking and collaboration among entrepreneurs, businesses, and other stakeholders. For instance, the centre will provide access to a network of mentors, investors, and industry experts, who can provide guidance and support to entrepreneurs and businesses.
